## Md Alomgeer Hussein

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at UMBC, working with Dr. Tera L. Reynolds in the CARE Research Lab. I build and evaluate large language model systems for patients' health questions.

### Recent work

- **Comparing Human and Large Language Model Responses to Patients' Online Questions** (AMIA Annual Symposium 2026). We compared four LLMs with peer answers to 122 laboratory-test questions. [Preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.15.26355314v1), [supplement](https://github.com/mdalomgeer/llm-vs-peer-ohc-supplementary)
- **HealthNLP_Retrievers at ArchEHR-QA 2026** (CL4Health @ LREC 2026). Our team placed first of 13 teams in question interpretation. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.63317/49k2p9jar95c)
- **TriAudit: Auditing and Improving Trustworthy LLMs for Health Consumers**. Under review at ACM TRUST 2027.

### Interests

LLM evaluation, responsible and trustworthy AI, health informatics, patient-facing AI, human-computer interaction.
